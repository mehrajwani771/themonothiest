About

I’m The Monotheist – a self-confessed failed student and a failed private teacher, still striving to revive moral, ethical and humanitarian values.

I teach whatever is placed in front of me at the moment, and I read and write on many subjects and essays. My aim is simple: every idea should start simply and build step by step, so that anyone willing to learn can follow it.
